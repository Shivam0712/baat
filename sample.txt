English: Hello
Translation: สวัสดี
Phonetics: sa-wat-dee
Note: Used when greeting someone. Add "khrap" if you are male or "kha" if you are female to make it polite. It can also be used when saying goodbye in formal situations.

English: Thank you
Translation: ขอบคุณ
Phonetics: khop-khun
Note: The standard way to say "thank you." Add "khrap" (male) or "kha" (female) at the end to sound more polite.

English: How much is this?
Translation: อันนี้เท่าไหร่
Phonetics: an-nee tao-rai
Note: Point at the item while asking. Very useful when shopping at markets, street stalls, or small shops.

English: Excuse me
Translation: ขอโทษ
Phonetics: kho-tot
Note: Used both to apologize and to politely get someone's attention before asking a question.

English: Water please
Translation: ขอน้ำหน่อย
Phonetics: kho-nam-noi
Note: "Noi" softens the request, making it sound more polite and natural. Commonly used at restaurants or when asking someone for a glass of water.

English: Where is the bathroom?
Translation: ห้องน้ำอยู่ที่ไหน
Phonetics: hong-nam-yu-tee-nai
Note: Literally means "Where is the water room?" This is one of the most useful phrases for travelers.

English: I don't understand
Translation: ไม่เข้าใจ
Phonetics: mai-kao-jai
Note: Means "I don't understand." Add "khrap" (male) or "kha" (female) to sound more polite when speaking to someone.

English: Delicious
Translation: อร่อย
Phonetics: a-roi
Note: Used to compliment food. Saying "a-roi mak" means "very delicious," and locals usually appreciate hearing it.
