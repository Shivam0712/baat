English: Hello
Translation: สวัสดี
Phonetics: sa-wat-dee
Note: Used when greeting someone. Add ครับ (khrap) if male, ค่ะ (kha) if female to be polite.

English: Thank you
Translation: ขอบคุณ
Phonetics: khop-khun
Note: Add ครับ or ค่ะ at the end for a more polite form.

English: How much is this?
Translation: อันนี้เท่าไหร่
Phonetics: an-nee tao-rai
Note: Point at the item when asking. Very useful when shopping at markets.

English: Excuse me
Translation: ขอโทษ
Phonetics: kho-tot
Note: Used both to apologize and to get someone's attention.

English: Water please
Translation: ขอน้ำหน่อย
Phonetics: kho-nam-noi
Note: หน่อย (noi) softens the request. Works at restaurants or when asking a host.

English: Where is the bathroom?
Translation: ห้องน้ำอยู่ที่ไหน
Phonetics: hong-nam-yu-tee-nai
Note: ห้องน้ำ literally means "water room". Essential phrase for travellers.

English: I don't understand
Translation: ไม่เข้าใจ
Phonetics: mai-kao-jai
Note: ไม่ (mai) means "no/not". You can also say ไม่เข้าใจครับ/ค่ะ to be polite.

English: Delicious
Translation: อร่อย
Phonetics: a-roi
Note: A great compliment to give after a meal. Locals love hearing this from visitors.
